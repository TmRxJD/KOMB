KOMB

The Klipper Optimized Macro for Brushing.

A flexible nozzle-brushing macro for Klipper. It works with bed-mounted, gantry-mounted and frame-mounted brushes, sweeps in three dimensions, and generates the whole tool path up front so every move is checked against your printer's travel limits before anything starts moving.

Get KOMB on GitHub →

Read this first. KOMB drives your nozzle around near your bed and brush. Measure carefully, dry-run in the air before letting the nozzle touch anything, and make sure position_min / position_max are set correctly for every axis.

Install

cd ~
git clone https://github.com/TmRxJD/KOMB
ln -s ~/KOMB ~/printer_data/config/KOMB
cp ~/KOMB/_KOMB_Variables.cfg ~/printer_data/config/KOMB_Variables.cfg

Then add [include KOMB_Variables.cfg] to your printer.cfg, edit that file to match your brush, and FIRMWARE_RESTART. Full instructions, including Moonraker update-manager setup, are in the README.

Features

Flexible movement. KOMB sweeps along X from the bottom-left corner by default, but you can sweep along either axis from either direction, and use it mid-print.

Two patterns. Diagonals at any angle from 0° (a straight wipe) to 90° (scrubbing in place), and circles (requires [gcode_arcs]). More to come.

Three-dimensional brushing. The nozzle can descend a little after each pass so more of its surface reaches the bristles, never going below the depth you allow.

Mid-print brushing. KOMB_TRIGGER runs the macro every N mm of print height from your slicer's layer-change G-code.

Purging. KOMB_PURGE extrudes a measured, volumetrically-specified blob into a purge bucket — handy before Z-offset probing.

Retraction control. Configure retraction before, during and after brushing, so even PETG stays put.

Safety by construction. Unlike macros that brute-force the nozzle to fixed coordinates, KOMB generates the entire sequence from your measurements and clamps every point to your configured axis limits before it commands a single move. Settings that cannot work are rejected up front with an explanation instead of failing halfway through.

Verbose feedback. Optional console output showing exactly what was calculated and where the nozzle is going.

Getting started

Home the printer and jog to the bottom-left corner of the brush for its X/Y location. Jog to the top-right and subtract to get its size. Jog down until the nozzle touches the bristles for the Z location, then decide how much further you will let it sink. Put those numbers in KOMB_Variables.cfg, restart Klipper, and run KOMB.

Every setting can also be overridden per call, for example KOMB REPEAT=3 SPEED=80 PATTERN=2.
